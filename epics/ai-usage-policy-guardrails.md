# EPIC: AI Usage Policy & Guardrails

## Goal
Enable enterprises to define, enforce, and audit acceptable AI usage across teams and applications.

## Problem Statement
AI systems behave inconsistently when guardrails are documented but not enforced at execution time.

## Success Metrics
- % of AI requests evaluated against policy
- Reduction in policy violations
- Time to audit AI behavior

## In Scope
- Policy definition
- Enforcement hooks
- Risk categorization

## Out of Scope
- Model training
- Prompt engineering optimization

## Dependencies
- Prompt validation module
- Audit logging framework

## Risks
- Over‑blocking useful responses
- Policy ambiguity
