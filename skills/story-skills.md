# PM Story Skill — AI Governance

## Purpose
Ensure every JIRA story preserves policy intent, risk context, and testability.

## Mandatory Story Context
- AI capability impacted
- Risk category (legal, ethical, security)
- Enforcement point (pre / during / post execution)

## Acceptance Criteria Rules
- Define allowed and disallowed behavior
- Include example prompts and outcomes
- Specify failure behavior

## Non‑Functional Expectations
- Decisions must be auditable
- Behavior must be explainable
- No silent failures

## Output Format
- JIRA‑ready
- Clear ownership
- Testable without model retraining
